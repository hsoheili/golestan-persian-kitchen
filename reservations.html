Reservations

We hold tables for two weeks out. Fill in the form below and one of us will call or email you back the same day — usually within a couple of hours, unless we are in the middle of a dinner rush. If you would rather just talk to a person, ring (604) 985-2147 between 11am and 9pm.

Request a table

Everything marked required helps us seat you properly. Tell us about allergies, a birthday, a high chair, or a guest who needs the wheelchair-accessible side of the room, and we will have it sorted before you arrive.

A request is not a confirmed booking until you hear back from us. If you have not heard anything by the end of the day, call the restaurant — our email filter has been known to swallow things.

When we are open

The kitchen takes its last order thirty minutes before close. The charcoal grill is lit for every service, lunch included.

Lunch, Tuesday to Friday
11:30am – 2:30pm. Quick kebab plates, chelo, and the daily khoresht. Most tables turn in under an hour if you tell us you are on a lunch break.
Dinner, Tuesday to Sunday
5:00pm – 9:30pm, and until 10:30pm on Friday and Saturday. Full menu, including the stews that need the long simmer.
Weekend brunch
Saturday and Sunday, 9:00am – 2:00pm. Barbari bread out of the oven every twenty minutes, haleem until it runs out.
Closed
Mondays, and for two weeks around Nowruz each spring. We post the exact dates on the door in February.

Parties of eight or more

Large tables are welcome, but they need a little planning. Call us rather than using the form so we can talk through the timing and what you want on the table.

  • Eight to fourteen guests

    We seat you at the long walnut table along the north wall. We ask for a set menu chosen three days ahead so the rice and the stews are timed to come out together instead of in a slow trickle.

  • The back room

    A separate room behind the dining room, sixteen seats at one table or twenty-four at three. No rental fee; there is a food and drink minimum of $650 at dinner and $400 at lunch. It has its own door, so a speech does not have to compete with the grill.

  • Deposit and confirmation

    Groups of twelve and up leave a $150 deposit, which comes off the final bill. We take it by card over the phone and refund it in full with five days' notice.

  • One bill, or several

    We will split a large table up to six ways. Beyond that, one bill is faster for everyone, including you. A 18% gratuity is added to parties of ten or more.

Walk-ins, waits, and cancellations

We keep about a third of the room open for people who simply walk in. On a Tuesday night you will sit down straight away. On a Saturday morning, you will not.

Saturday brunch, 10am to noon
Expect thirty to fifty minutes without a booking. Give us your mobile number at the door and go walk the Quay — we will text when your table is ready.
Best walk-in times
Before 9:45am or after 1:00pm on weekends. Weeknights before 6:00pm or after 8:15pm.
Holding your table
We hold a booked table for fifteen minutes. Call if the bridge is backed up and we will hold it longer — traffic on Lions Gate is not your fault.
Cancellation window
Tables of one to seven: let us know any time before your booking. Eight or more: twenty-four hours, please, since we will have shopped for you. Back room bookings: five days for a full deposit refund.

Finding us

We are at 1247 Lonsdale Avenue, on the east side between 12th and 13th, with a red awning and a samovar in the window.

Parking

Metered street parking runs along Lonsdale and is free after 8:00pm and all day Sunday. The side streets east of Lonsdale — 12th, 13th, and St Georges — are unmetered and usually have space within a block. There is a City lot on 13th Street just west of Lonsdale, a two-minute walk, flat rate after 6:00pm.

Transit from Lonsdale Quay

From the SeaBus terminal at Lonsdale Quay, take the 229 or the R2 Marine Drive up Lonsdale and get off at 13th Street. It is about eight minutes on the bus and a one-block walk south. Walking the whole way from the Quay takes roughly twenty-five minutes and is uphill the entire time, which is worth knowing before you start.

Getting in the door

Street-level entrance with no step, a door wide enough for a wheelchair, and an accessible washroom at the back past the private room. High chairs and booster seats are by the host stand — ask and we will bring one over.
